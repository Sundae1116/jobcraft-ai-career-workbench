# JobCraft product brief

## Mission

Turn a single verified career history into role-specific, evidence-backed job applications while keeping the candidate in control.

## Primary demo story

1. A public job is discovered and normalized.
2. Hard constraints remove bad fits before AI is used.
3. The system explains a weighted fit score.
4. One canonical evidence library produces a role-specific resume narrative.
5. A separate reviewer checks factuality, relevance, tone, and ATS readability.
6. Browser assistance prepares the application and stops at the human submit gate.
7. Outcomes feed an auditable application history.

## Initial role narratives

- AI Product Operations: growth loops, launch operations, user segmentation, adoption, and data-driven iteration.
- AI Product Experience: AI interaction patterns, trust and control, user research, usability, and feedback loops.

These are separate resume narratives backed by the same candidate facts. They are not separate identities and may not contradict each other.

## MVP boundary

The first usable release accepts a pasted job description, evaluates it, chooses a target narrative, produces a reviewed resume draft, and creates a tracked application. Real portal automation follows after the core evidence and review path is stable.
